## Description

Goalify Companion is the optional Windows desktop companion for the Goalify
addon on WoW Forever. The addon manages your goals in game; the companion
helps share permitted discoveries to improve community source data.

Choose your World of Warcraft folder and read observations saved by Goalify
after logout or /reload. Contribute supported vendor, loot-source, trainer,
NPC service, guard-direction and flight-node observations.

You control sharing. Enable Community Sharing in Goalify and give separate
consent for each account. Choose automatic sending while the companion is
open, or preview each batch and send it manually. Pause sharing at any time
or request deletion of this installation's shared data.

The companion does not modify game files or automate gameplay. Goals, gold
balances, inventory, mail, character GUIDs and account folder names are not
included in shared observations. An optional contributor display name is
shared only if you choose to save one.

Requires the Goalify addon, installed separately. Internet access is required
for sharing.

Independent community project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard
Entertainment.

## Screenshots

Upload installed-app captures under **Desktop**. Microsoft requires at least
one; four are recommended. Use PNG, at least 1366 x 768 pixels, no more than
50 MB each. Prefer 1920 x 1080 if the display supports it.

Do not stretch or upscale a small screenshot to claim a larger capture.
Avoid browser chrome, other apps, private account names, real install IDs,
mail, character names and credentials. Use the app's masked account labels.
Do not add marketing banners, extra logos or claims of Microsoft approval.
For preview captures, select manual sending and do not click Send merely to
create a screenshot.

Recommended order and captions (each caption is under 200 characters):

1. **Welcome and accounts**
   - Capture the version, Welcome and account summary.
   - Caption: "Choose your WoW folder and manage account-by-account sharing consent."

   - Open Preview for a permitted saved account; show the readable cards.
   - Caption: "Review supported observations before sending, with an optional JSON view."
3. **Sharing settings**
   - Show pause sharing and the automatic/manual sending choices. Keep any
     real install ID outside the captured area.
   - Caption: "Choose automatic or manual sending, and pause sharing whenever you need."
4. **About and updates**
   - Show the installed version and About & updates.
   - Caption: "See the installed version and open Microsoft Store to check for updates."
